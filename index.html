What this experiment calculates

For each UTC day, B is the average absolute open-to-close movement over the prior 10 completed daily candles. Support is today's open minus B; resistance is today's open plus B. The slider adds a half-width of 0-50% of B to each band. Today's unfinished daily candle is excluded.

Only the 10-day open/close basis comes from the public PP description. The mean, current-open anchor, symmetry, scale and band width are our own assumptions. Levels stay fixed during each UTC day. This is daily only; it does not infer PP's weekly or monthly rules.

Data comes directly from Coinbase, not OANDA or Exness. Broker prices, daily sessions and resulting levels can differ. The live ticker updates the latest hourly candle from received prices, not every individual trade. Disconnects may leave gaps; click Load latest again to replace the candles. This is not a broker-grade consolidated feed.

No accounts, keys, orders or payments. Loading latest makes two public data requests. Live prices use Coinbase's public WebSocket ticker. No background polling or automatic reconnection. If no update arrives for 20 seconds, the display says stale. Closing the tab stops the connection.
